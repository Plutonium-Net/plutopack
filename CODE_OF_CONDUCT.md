# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making PlutoPack a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Whether you are reporting a bug, proposing a feature, improving documentation, or contributing code, you are expected to participate respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate toward other contributors.
* Giving constructive feedback.
* Accepting differing opinions and technical approaches.
* Focusing criticism on ideas, code, or proposals rather than individuals.
* Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
* Respecting the project's maintainers, contributors, and users.
* Keeping discussions relevant and productive.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Deliberate trolling or disruptive behavior.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Sexualized comments or unwanted attention.
* Hate speech or discriminatory language.
* Deliberately disrupting development or community discussions.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably make the project unsafe or hostile for others.

## Technical Discussions

PlutoPack is a technical project, and disagreements about implementation, design, or the specification are expected.

Technical disagreement is not inherently disrespectful. Contributors are encouraged to challenge ideas, identify problems, and propose alternatives.

When disagreements occur, keep the discussion focused on the technical issue rather than the person proposing it.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

* Asking a participant to stop inappropriate behavior.
* Removing comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions.
* Temporarily restricting participation in project discussions.
* Permanently restricting participation in the project.

Enforcement decisions may take into account the severity, frequency, and circumstances of the behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers.

For security-related issues, please follow the instructions in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead.

When making a report, provide as much relevant information as possible, including links or other evidence when available.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

* The PlutoPack repository.
* Project discussions, issues, and pull requests.
* Project-related communication and community spaces.
* Anyone participating in the project in an official capacity.
